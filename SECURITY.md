# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability
We take the safety of our users and beneficiaries extremely seriously. If you discover a vulnerability:

1. **Do NOT** open a public GitHub issue.
2. Email your findings to: **security@sahaaye.org**
3. Include:
   - Detailed description of the vulnerability.
   - Proof-of-concept (PoC) or reproducible steps.
   - Potential impact on users, beneficiaries, or infrastructure.
